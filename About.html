What I bring to your team

Complex made simple

I take data-heavy products like CRMs, merchant tools and internal platforms, and turn them into interfaces people find clear and easy to use.

Global experience

Years of working with teams and clients in the U.S. and Europe, remote and on-site, across time zones and cultures.

Research to release

User interviews and usability tests, user flows, interaction design, prototypes and developer handoff. I can own a feature from the first question to the final release.

Developers and stakeholders
