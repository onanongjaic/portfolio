03NANO VIP

Nano VIP: booking a chauffeur-driven car in a few taps

A mobile app for a premium car and driver service in Bangkok, from choosing a car to a confirmed, paid ride.

Company
Nano Stores · Nano VIP
My role
UX/UI Designer
Timeline
Sep 2025 to Feb 2026
Platform
Mobile app

Overview

Nano VIP offers chauffeur-driven cars in Bangkok, by the hour or as airport and city transfers. Booking one usually meant calls and chats about time, bags, a child seat or drinks. I designed the customer app so all of this fits in a few short steps that still feel premium.

The problem

The existing UI was difficult to use, making the booking process less intuitive for users. I redesigned the interface to improve usability, simplify navigation, and create a smoother booking experience across the platform.

Process

I kept every step short and familiar. Sign in takes a Thai phone number or one tap with Apple or Google, and guests can still book first and sign in later. Before booking, people can look inside each car with interior photos and a rotating view, and compare models by seats, luggage and price.

Why guest booking?

I introduced a guest booking option to reduce friction in the booking process. Users can make a reservation without creating an account, allowing them to complete their booking more quickly and conveniently.

The solution

A dark and gold visual language matches the brand and the cars. Home brings services, the vehicle catalogue and deals together, one central Book button starts a booking from any tab, and payment shows clear progress so no one leaves halfway.

Booking flow

Six short steps from where to go to a confirmed ride. Each screen asks one clear thing, and extras like luggage and child seats are simple counters with the price shown up front.

What I learned

A premium feel comes from confidence, not decoration. Showing the inside of each car before booking and clear progress during payment answer the questions people would otherwise call to ask. Letting guests book first and sign in later also taught me to never let the account get in the way of the ride.
