02SUPER PETS SHOP

Super Pets Shop: one pet store across web and app

An e-commerce website and mobile app for pet owners in Thailand.

Company
Nano Stores · Super Pets Shop
My role
UX/UI Designer
Timeline
Sep 2025 to Feb 2026
Platform
Website, mobile app

Website homepage

A promo banner with one clear offer, then shop by category and brand, recommended products with stock states, and a free delivery offer to bring new members in.

Overview

Super Pets Shop sells food, toys, care products and accessories for dogs and cats. I designed the website and the mobile app, keeping browsing quick across a long catalogue of brands, sizes and offers.

Live websiteSee the shop running at superpetshop.com.
Visit the website ↗

The problem

The existing shop was hard to use, and some features the business needed did not exist yet. The MD and team lead asked for a company website to sell products online, with a complete flow covering every step of buying. Starting from those requirements, I redesigned the existing pages and designed the missing features as new: the user flows from browsing products to checkout and order tracking, and the UI for the website and the mobile app.

The design challenge

Create a consistent shopping experience across web and mobile while supporting a large product catalogue with different brands, sizes, prices, and stock availability.

Design approach

I started by studying example e-commerce websites, then adapted the patterns that fit our products, our systems and the company. From there I designed platform-specific components and interactions to make product discovery, purchasing, and order tracking easier across different screen sizes.

Key UX decisions

  • Clear product variants, pricing, and stock availability, so shoppers know exactly what they are buying before they add it to the cart.
  • Consistent product states across web and mobile, so customers who switch devices see the same labels and do not have to relearn the shop.
  • Transparent shipping options and additional charges, so there are no surprise costs at the last step of checkout.
  • Clear order and delivery status tracking, so customers can follow their order themselves instead of contacting the shop.

Process

The website and the app each have their own components, designed for how people shop on that screen. Clear states such as new, discount, out of stock and order status help people decide and follow up at a glance.

Mobile

On mobile, the shop keeps the same product states, prices and stock labels as the website, laid out in a single column for smaller screens.

The solution

A bright orange brand with friendly pet mascots keeps the shop warm and easy to scan. Checkout offers standard or same-day delivery, a choice of shipping providers, coupons, and card or Thai QR payment.

What I learned

Small details like stock status and the size on each review matter a lot when people are buying for a pet they love. Designing the website and the app separately also taught me to plan each one around how people really use that screen.
