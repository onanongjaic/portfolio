04THAINOW

ThaiNow: a community platform for Thai people in the U.S.

A mobile app that helps Thai people living in the U.S. find local deals, housing, jobs and Thai-owned businesses nearby, through an interactive map or list.

Company
Project ThaiHub Inc. · ThaiNow
My role
UX/UI Designer
Timeline
2021 to 2022
Platform
Mobile app

Overview

ThaiNow is a community platform designed for Thai people living in the U.S. It helps users discover local deals, find housing and job opportunities, and buy or sell items within the Thai community. I redesigned the core experience to make it easier for users to explore relevant listings, discover nearby businesses, and find what they need through a more intuitive and accessible interface.

Process

I started by interviewing real users to understand their needs, and mapped the user journey from opening the app to discovering and using a deal. Then, I translated the journey into user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs in Figma, and ran usability tests with real users to validate the design. I also designed synchronized map and list views, allowing users to explore nearby businesses geographically or compare available offers in a list.

The solution

Every deal card shows the old and new price, the discount, the rating and whether it is still available. The deal page adds the location with directions and customer reviews, so people can decide and go in one place.

What I learned

Designing for my own community taught me to start from people's real situation: where they are and what they need in that moment. It is also where I learned to carry a feature all the way from user flow to prototype to handoff.
